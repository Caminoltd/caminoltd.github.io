# Skillable lab help

<p class="lead">Applies to all Camino Ltd Check Point courses.</p>

## Registering and getting into your lab

1. Register at <https://arrowecs-com.learnondemand.net> using the Training Key in your email.
2. Keep a note of your Skillable username and password. You will need them during the course and afterwards.
3. Your lab stays available for **180 days** after the course ends.

## Keyboard and timezone

The lab machines need to match your keyboard and timezone.
The [lab preparation checklist](../lab-preparation/) explains the quick way to change them. The full illustrated guide is here:
