# CCSE R82 – Lab preparation

<p class="lead">Please complete this before the first lab of the course.</p>

## 1. Register with Skillable

1. Go to <https://arrowecs-com.learnondemand.net> and register using the **Training Key** from your email.
2. Keep a note of your Skillable username and password.

## 2. Prepare your lab (before the first lab)

1. **Launch the lab** at <https://arrowecs-com.learnondemand.net>.
2. Go to the **RDP-HOST** virtual machine. It is the last VM in the list, so scroll down to find it.
3. On RDP-HOST, open **File Explorer**, find **`C:\scripts\changes.cmd`** and run it.
4. Choose your options in each of the **4 windows**, clicking **OK** after each one.
5. **Wait for all VMs to reboot.** This takes 3 to 5 minutes.
6. **Start Lab 1A.**
